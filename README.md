# Robinson Crusoe — The Island Journal

A local React / TypeScript / Phaser 3 reconstruction of Exkee's **Robinson Crusoe: Shipwrecked**, built from the supplied Java ME JAR. The current milestone adds recovered six-minute tides, original shore recovery and low-tide tool access, swamp-raft repair, paddling, landing and transport saves, Pirate ship reconstruction, Large sail crafting and the timed escape ending, recovered direct world-food consumption, nutrition-driven body weight and condition details, world-contact animal hazards, 16 craftable garments, directional outfit rendering and hunting protection, house furnishings and real-time sleep recovery, persistent Tent/Cabin upgrades and recovered crafting tiers, bow hunting, rod fishing, shallow-water net fishing, coconut throwing, survival, food use, rest, wildlife behavior and offline reload; it is not yet the complete original adventure.

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the URL printed by Vite. Choose **Begin expedition**, or **Continue a saved story**. WASD/arrows move, click walks using pathfinding, E examines a nearby object. I opens inventory, C crafting, J journal, M map, Escape pauses. Zoom controls are on the right. Save from the top bar or pause menu; IndexedDB stores your expedition on this device. New expeditions use recovered hunger/thirst/energy depletion. Eat from the satchel, or choose **Eat … here** from a nearby food discovery with the required tool. Press R (or the moon button) for a ten-second rest. Settings can disable survival for exploration. Earlier saves remain in exploration mode until enabled. Nearby predators now drain energy and interrupt rest; matching clothing halves their loss. Sharks exhaust energy instantly. Rest in a safe place. The condition shortcut shows Strength, Morale and nutrition-driven Weight; Weight does not impose a carrying limit.

Implemented: original 180×180 island, exact packed atlas frames/anchors, reconstructed vegetation, recovered Robinson idle/walking sprites, collision, camera, inspection, loose-resource pickup, inventory categories and 21 original supplies/equipment recipes and two in-place camp upgrades plus four house furnishings and 16 garments with reusable tools and recovered shelter-tier requirements, chart/journal, saves, and 326 moving animals across 16 recovered species. Birds take flight, prey flees, predators pursue, and sharks stay in water; world-contact hazards use recovered proximity, timing and clothing checks. Bow hunting supports 15 recovered species, apex shots, source rewards/ammunition costs and stable wildlife removal; dangerous hunts can retaliate. Food/tool effects, pickup/crafting energy costs, short-rest sprites and persistent survival state are also playable. Carry a stone to a coconut-bearing palm (one is at island grid 28,160) to play the recovered two-hit, ten-throw challenge; a win awards one coconut. Inspect shallow water with a crafted Net and Potato bait to play the original ten-fish/five-cast net game. Inspect deep water with a Fishing rod and Worms for the recovered trailing-hook/reeling game. Build Tent in the workbench with Branch×3, Sail×1 and a kept Knife to unlock the next recipe tier. Upgrade to Cabin at the same original camp (24,129) for Cabin-tier recipes. Camp location and collision stay intact. Make Single bed, Barrel, Rug and Double bed with their original recipes; inspect them in House storage from the camp or workbench. Settings offers Sleep (save and return to title): after at least one real hour away, Continue restores the recovered reserve bonus once. Ordinary saves retain paused reserves. Open Change clothes from the satchel or Your wardrobe from the workbench to equip crafted garments. Original BS5 layers animate with Robinson, and the appropriate worn slot halves each dangerous species’ hunting retaliation. The same clothing protection applies to timed world contacts; sharks remain immediately fatal to energy. Optional ocean ambience and atmospheric clock/lighting are browser enhancements.

Make the Large sail in the workbench, then reconstruct the original pirate ship with its six material groups and six kept tools for the timed escape ending. **A complete new-game-to-escape route is still unfinished:** the wreck and Sextant require the combined raft route in the optimistic access audit. Swamp raft traversal and changing tides are now supported; the complete material and access route remains pending. See [ship reconstruction and access audit](docs/SHIP_RECONSTRUCTION.md), [raft traversal](docs/RAFT_TRAVERSAL.md), and [tide recovery](docs/TIDES.md). Ending QA supplies materials and positions Robinson beside the unchanged original wreck.

Offline reload: production now uses one classic browser script (including styles), avoiding the earlier offline module-loading failure. After the first complete load and the “Island assets cached” indicator, the game can reload without its local server. Development mode still needs its dev server. This was verified in the embedded browser; other browser families need further QA.

Saves belong to the browser URL’s origin. Return using the same hostname and port to resume the same expedition; `localhost` and `127.0.0.1` have separate saves.
